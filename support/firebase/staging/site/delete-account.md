## In the app

1. Open Stepstones and sign in.
2. Go to your profile and open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete Profile** and confirm.
4. For security, you may be asked to sign in again with the same account.

Your account is deleted right away, and your data is removed from our servers within minutes. This cannot be undone.

## By email

If you can’t use the app, email <span class="placeholder">[CONTACT EMAIL]</span> from the address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email, name and sign-in method)
- Your profile (name, email, settings, home city, statistics)
- Your profile photo
- Your practice session history
- Your “practicing now” presence entry

## What is kept

- Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example total minutes practiced by all users), which can’t be linked to you.
- Analytics and crash data, which are deleted automatically after their retention period (see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy)).
- Mindful minutes already written to Apple Health or Health Connect. These are stored on your device and in your Apple or Google account; you can remove them there.
